Payment advice

A row is ready the moment the HOD approves that person’s invoice. Preview reads it and Edit corrects it; nothing has to be written or sent, because Download has it either way.

?Payment advice guide
  1. Preview an approved invoice’s row — it is already filled in from the invoice.
  2. Edit and save it if something on it needs to be different — every box on the form can be typed over.
  3. Print it from Download for the HOD to sign.
  4. File the signed copy in Re-Upload.

No further approval is needed in this app. Payment advice is not shown to consultants.

Send the month to Finance

One zip of everything signed for the month, and the e-mail that carries it. Download zip keeps a copy; Email Finance opens the message already written, with the zip attached.

?Sending guide

The table says what the zip will hold: a signed copy where one was filed on the Re-Upload step, and the document drawn from the app where one was not. Anything missing is named, so a month is never sent short without it being said. Email Finance downloads an .eml file — open it and it becomes a draft in Outlook, addressed and written, with the zip attached. Nothing is sent until you press Send there.

Filed claim forms

Signed claims sent to Group People & Finance, with monthly leave records.

Re-upload the signed copies

Attach the signed time sheet and payment advice; the approved invoice is added automatically. One file a document, saved the moment you choose it — uploading again replaces it. Submit closes each month as soon as its own copies are in; the rest stay open.

?Upload guide

Match each copy to its person, month and document type, and check the preview. A file is on the record the moment you choose it, and those months stay open, so a scan is safe the day it arrives. Submit closes every month whose time sheet and payment advice both have a copy, and leaves the others open until theirs arrive. A reopened month says Resubmit.

Uploading a new scan replaces the existing filed copy.
