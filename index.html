Download for signature

Download the approved claim forms and arrange the HOD’s signature. Then open Upload to attach and submit the signed copies.

Payment Advice

Uzma’s own form for paying a consultant’s invoice. Everybody is listed below; a row unlocks once that person has sent their invoice for the month. Open it, fill in the office’s own boxes, and send it round the way a time sheet goes — the project manager, the HOD, then back here to be signed. The consultant never sees this form.

Filed claim forms

Signed claim forms sent to Group People & Finance, newest first. Each record includes the leave recorded on that month’s form.

Upload the signed copy

Attach each signed claim form and preview it. When everything is ready, use Submit below to send the copies to Group People & Finance. Uploading a newer scan replaces the filed copy.
