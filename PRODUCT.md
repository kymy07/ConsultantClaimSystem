# Consultant Claim System

## Register

product

## Users

Consultants prepare monthly claims and invoices. Project managers and the HOD
review submissions. The PA prepares payment advice and files signed copies.
Administrators manage profiles, numbering, leave allowances and records.

## Product Purpose

Keep monthly document preparation, approvals and signed records in one workspace.
Preserve the official claim, invoice and payment advice templates and calculations.

## Brand Personality

Clear, practical and professional. Retain the existing Geospatial AI identity,
English interface, slate/navy palette and orange actions.

## Anti-references

Avoid long instructions above every task, undifferentiated form fields and
competing actions. The user requests a simpler interface without changing its base.

## Design Principles

- Group fields by the task they serve: personal details, payment and signature.
- Keep essential actions, balances and warnings visible.
- Put optional explanations behind labelled, expandable help controls.
- Separate preparing documents, submitting them and reviewing records.
- Preserve permissions, saved data and exported document formats.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Retain labelled inputs, visible keyboard focus, responsive layouts and reduced
motion support. Help must work with touch and keyboard, with a readable label
alongside its symbol. Official document previews scroll within their own area.
